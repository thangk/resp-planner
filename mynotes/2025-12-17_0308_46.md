# Frontload Calculation

## What is Frontloading?

Frontloading means contributing more than the CESG-eligible amount in a single year. The extra money doesn't earn government grants, but it compounds tax-free for longer — maximizing growth.

---

## The Formula

```
Frontload Amount = Lifetime Limit − Prior Contributions − Future CESG Contributions
```

Where:

- **Lifetime Limit** = $50,000 per child
- **Prior Contributions** = Total already contributed before the frontload year
- **Future CESG Contributions** = Minimum contributions needed in years after frontload to capture remaining CESG grants

---

## Step-by-Step Calculation

### Step 1: Calculate Remaining Room

```
Remaining Room = $50,000 − Prior Contributions
```

### Step 2: Determine CESG-Eligible Amount for Frontload Year

Based on available contribution room:

- If catch-up room exists: up to **$5,000** (earns $1,000 CESG)
- If no catch-up room: **$2,500** (earns $500 CESG)

### Step 3: Calculate Future CESG Contributions

For each year from (frontload year + 1) until the child turns 17:

| Situation                 | Annual Contribution | CESG Earned |
| ------------------------- | ------------------- | ----------- |
| Catch-up room available   | $5,000              | $1,000      |
| No catch-up room          | $2,500              | $500        |
| CESG lifetime cap reached | $0 (optional)       | $0          |

Sum all future contributions needed to max out the $7,200 CESG lifetime cap.

### Step 4: Calculate Frontload

```
Frontload = Remaining Room − CESG-Eligible (frontload year) − Future CESG Contributions
```

### Step 5: Total Frontload Year Contribution

```
Total Contribution = CESG-Eligible Amount + Frontload Amount
```

## Example: Rachel

**Given:**

- Born: September 2022
- Frontload year: 2028
- Prior contributions (2025-2027): $6,000
- Prior CESG received: $1,200

**Calculation:**

| Item                | Amount      |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Lifetime limit      | $50,000     |
| Prior contributions | −$6,000     |
| **Remaining room**  | **$44,000** |

**Future CESG contributions (2029-2034):**

| Year      | Contribution | CESG   | Notes               |
| --------- | ------------ | ------ | ------------------- |
| 2029      | $5,000       | $1,000 | Catch-up            |
| 2030      | $5,000       | $1,000 | Catch-up            |
| 2031      | $5,000       | $1,000 | Catch-up            |
| 2032      | $2,500       | $500   |                     |
| 2033      | $2,500       | $500   |                     |
| 2034      | $1,750       | $350   | Partial (hits $50K) |
| **Total** | **$21,750**  | $4,350 |                     |

**Frontload calculation:**

```
2028 Contribution = $44,000 − $21,750 = $22,250
```

**Breakdown of $22,250:**

- $5,000 → CESG-eligible (earns $1,000)
- $17,250 → Pure frontload (no grants, just growth)

## Example: Hannah

**Given:**

- Born: June 2025
- Frontload year: 2028
- Prior contributions (2025-2027): $6,000
- Prior CESG received: $1,200

**Calculation:**

| Item                | Amount      |
| ------------------- | ----------- |
| Lifetime limit      | $50,000     |
| Prior contributions | −$6,000     |
| **Remaining room**  | **$44,000** |

**Future CESG contributions (2029-2037):**

| Year      | Contribution          | CESG      | Notes            |
| --------- | --------------------- | --------- | ---------------- |
| 2029-2037 | $2,500/year × 9 years | $500/year | No catch-up room |
| **Total** | **$22,500**           | $4,500    |                  |

**Frontload calculation:**

```
2028 Contribution = $44,000 − $22,500 = $21,500
```

**Breakdown of $21,500:**

- $4,000 → CESG-eligible (earns $800, partial catch-up)
- $17,500 → Pure frontload (no grants, just growth)

---

## Why Hannah Has Less Catch-Up Room

| Child  | Birth Year | Years of Room by 2028 | Room Accumulated | Room Used | Available |
| ------ | ---------- | --------------------- | ---------------- | --------- | --------- |
| Rachel | 2022       | 7 years               | $17,500          | $6,000    | $11,500   |
| Hannah | 2025       | 4 years               | $10,000          | $6,000    | $4,000    |

Rachel has 3+ years of catch-up room, so she can contribute $5,000/year for several years.
Hannah only has $1,500 of catch-up room ($4,000 − $2,500 current year), so she maxes out catch-up in 2028.

## Summary

| Child  | Prior  | Frontload Year | Future CESG | Frontload Amount | CESG in Frontload Year |
| ------ | ------ | -------------- | ----------- | ---------------- | ---------------------- |
| Rachel | $6,000 | 2028           | $21,750     | $22,250          | $1,000                 |
| Hannah | $6,000 | 2028           | $22,500     | $21,500          | $800                   |
